### Why Spin?

Spin plays a fundamental role in nature.

My interest in spin began with quantum mechanics, where I first learned that spin is an intrinsic property of elementary particles. I became increasingly fascinated by the many ways spin shapes the physical world. Phenomena such as the Pauli exclusion principle, the structure of the periodic table, magnetism, and quantum statistics all arise from the distinction between fermions and bosons.

The deeper I studied these topics, the more I realized how profoundly spin influences our understanding of matter. This motivated me to explore spin-related problems during my PhD research.


### Proton Spin Decomposition

The proton is one of the fundamental building blocks of visible matter. Understanding its internal structure is therefore an important step toward understanding the physical world itself. From a reductionist perspective, studying the proton provides a way to connect the rich complexity of nature to the underlying dynamics of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD).
